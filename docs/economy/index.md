# Economy

Money matters more than it seems early on. You'll spend it constantly while leveling, and costs rise as the game goes on.

## Currency

Prado uses a simple base-100 system:

| | Equals |
|---|---|
| 100 Copper | 1 Silver |
| 100 Silver | 1 Gold |

## What money is for

- **Spellbooks and gear** from town stores while leveling
- **Crafting materials**, most importantly the **crystals** every recipe needs
- **Resting** in town to heal without using items

!!! warning "Don't go broke"
    The cost of resting goes up as the game goes on. Running out of money means healing with items or not at all, so always keep a buffer.

## Trading Post :lucide-triangle-alert:{ .lone-wolf-warn title="Ends Lone Wolf status" }

The Trading Post lets players buy and sell with each other. It unlocks once you reach **Eastern Gate**, and there are more Trading Posts in **Gloomhaven** and **Stormwatch**.

Almost everything can be traded: weapons, armor, spellbooks, crafting recipes, potions, food, gems, runes, waystones, and even junk.

### Stackable items

Crystals, ore, cloth, leather, food, spellbooks, and anything else that stacks are traded in bulk through buy and sell orders.

- **Buy now:** take the lowest existing sell order.
- **Sell now:** fill the highest existing buy order.
- **Place a buy order:** set your price and put up a gold deposit.
- **Place a sell order:** list your items at the price you choose.

### Non-stackable items

Weapons, armor, waystones, and runes are each unique, so every one gets its own listing with a set price. There's no bidding. Buyers can filter by item level, stat requirements, enchantments, sockets, and more.

### Fees

The seller always pays the fee.

| How you sell | Fee |
|---|---|
| List an item for sale | 3 to 5% |
| Sell instantly into a buy order | 10% |

!!! tip "List instead of selling instantly"
    Listing your own sell order costs far less than selling into an existing buy order. Unless you need the money right away, list it.

### Mailbox

Items and gold from the Trading Post are delivered to your **Mailbox**. Gold from a sale always arrives **two hours** after the sale, which gives the developers time to catch suspicious trades.

!!! warning "Lone Wolf players"
    Using the Trading Post ends your [Lone Wolf](../character-creation/index.md#lone-wolf-optional) status.
