# FAQ

Click a question to see the answer.

??? question "Do steps count when the app is closed?"
    Yes. Prado reads your steps from your phone's built-in step counter, so the next time you open the app, you'll get VITA for up to two days of walking since you last played.

??? question "Is there a limit to how much VITA I can save up?"
    Yes. You can hold up to **80 VITA** at a time. If your steps earned you more than that, the extra waits for you and gets added as you spend what you have.

??? question "What happens if my character dies partway through a deck?"
    If your whole party is defeated, you're sent back to town and the run counts as a loss. You keep any loot you've picked up, but the VITA is gone.

    If only some of your party goes down, they come back with 1 health after the fight and keep going.

??? question "What's the level cap?"
    Currently **25** for your main class and **12** for your subclass. See [Subclass level](../classes/index.md#subclass-level) for how subclass levels work.

??? question "What happens to my Seasonal character when the season ends?"
    It moves to the Legacy server, and your journey continues there. See [Seasonal or Legacy](../character-creation/index.md#seasonal-or-legacy).

??? question "What does a subscription get me, and is it pay to win?"
    Subscribers get:

    - The **Bard**, **Blood Knight**, and the current season's **Ranger**
    - Double inventory and stash space (**200** instead of 100)
    - **2 extra attunements** on every item when enchanting
    - Season-exclusive cosmetics
    - A Discord role with access to private channels

    It's definitely not pay to win. The free classes can beat everything in the game and have plenty of powerful builds of their own. A subscription is about quality of life and variety.

??? question "Is there a faster way to open treasure chests?"
    Yes. Instead of opening a chest and then clicking and swiping each item one at a time:

    1. Open your **stash**.
    2. Scroll down to the treasure chest in your inventory and open it.
    3. Wait 2 to 3 seconds. All the items resolve on their own.

    It doesn't change what you get, but it saves a lot of tapping on chests with 8 or more items.
