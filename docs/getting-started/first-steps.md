# First Steps

Character made? You'll start in Sweetwater. Here's how a first session usually goes, and each step links to the full guide when you want to go deeper.

## 1. Open your starting packs

Check your inventory for packs that can be opened. There should be one with some health and mana recovery food. Be sure to open the pack from your friend code too (unless you skipped it) and equip your **Ring of Friendship**.

## 2. Aim for 5,000 steps a day

Your first 5,000 steps each day earn the most VITA, plus 200 bonus experience. Making it a daily habit from the start is the easiest way to keep progressing.

[:lucide-arrow-right: How VITA works](core-concepts.md#vita)

## 3. Get your first ability

**Pebble Beach** is the level 1 adventure deck, so start there. Crooked Creek will open up, but I recommend running the **Forage at Lake Serentis** gathering deck to make sure you reach level 2 and pick up some materials you can sell in town.

!!! warning "Never sell your crystals"
    Every crafting recipe needs at least one crystal, and you'll go through a lot of them. Hold onto every crystal you find, even if you're not crafting yet. You'll be glad you did.

The goal is to get your first ability as soon as possible. Abilities are learned from spellbooks, which you can find as random monster drops or buy from the **Skillbooks** tab in certain stores, like the **Sunflower Market** in Sweetwater. Click a spellbook and check its class tag to make sure it's for your class.

To learn and equip it:

1. Find the spellbook in the **Consumables** tab of your Inventory and click **Consume Item**.
2. Go to **Profile**, then **Traits & Abilities** at the bottom, and click an empty ability slot.
3. Click the ability you want and click **Equip** to add it to your active abilities.

Now you can spend your mana (or health, for a Blood Knight) on powerful attacks that make monsters much easier to take down. Warriors have a unique option here: **Rage** powers up each consecutive swing and costs nothing to keep active, all the time.

Keep an eye out for the Honeyglow Bear in Whispering Caves, since its Uniques are a nice early boost.

## 4. Get geared

It's important to fill all your gear slots as fast as possible, with your weapon as the top priority. Pay attention to monster drops and equip things as they drop, or fill any empty slots with gear from the store.

If you've been lucky enough to find some Wind Crystals, seriously consider Tailoring some armor to get started. Crafted gear always starts as Uncommon with both affix slots filled, so it's much stronger than the Common gear in the store.

[:lucide-arrow-right: Gearing Up](../gearing-up/index.md)

## 5. Rest and restock in town

Rest at the **Riverbend Tavern** to recover health and mana between runs, and stock up on food at the **Sunflower Market**. Keep in mind that leveling up instantly refills your health and mana, so if you're close to your next level, push through instead of resting and save yourself some coins.

[:lucide-arrow-right: Economy](../economy/index.md)

## 6. Head to Westfield at level 5

**Travel to Westfield** takes you to the next town, with farmland decks and the Old Mill dungeon. Be warned, though: Westfield has no Alchemy station, so brew any potions you'll need before you head out.

!!! warning "Common early mistakes"
    - **Leaving your ability on all the time.** Early on, your mana can't keep up with it. Try using it for the first fight of a deck, then turning it off until you've recovered, so you don't run out of gas halfway through. This gets important once you reach double-deckers like Old Mill Dungeon into The Miller's Vault, which ends with a powerful Unique Monster "boss" fight.
    - **Spending every last coin.** Resting gets more expensive as you go, so keep a buffer.
    - **Deleting a Seasonal character on a whim.** It can lock you out of Seasonal for 30 days.
