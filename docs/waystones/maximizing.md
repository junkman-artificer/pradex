# Maximizing Waystones

Getting the most out of every waystone run.

For how party size affects gear drops and VITA costs, see [Party Size and Drops](../parties/party-size.md).

## The idea

Uniques only show up on the last card of each level, starting from the second level. The first level never has one. Every card before that costs you time and VITA, so **the smaller the deck, the more shots at Uniques you get for the same VITA**. Most of maximizing comes down to keeping your decks small and making the cards you do add count.

## Sorting your runes

You'll pick up a lot of runes, and most aren't worth keeping. A quick way to sort them:

**Keep**

- Runes that add 2 to 3 Wild cards.
- Runes that add 2 cards, as long as they aren't both Monster.
- Runes that turn Wild cards into Treasure or Shrine cards.
- Any +Treasure or +Shrine rune.

**Sell**

- Runes that add 4 cards.
- Runes that add 3 cards when 2 or more of them are Monster.

**It depends**

- **2 Monster cards:** usually a sell, unless its Item Drop Rate or Item Rarity Fortune is Tier IV or V. Those are worth keeping for a Monster hunting deck.
- **Mana Cost or Regen Reduction Omens:** some players can't stand them, and others don't mind. Decide based on how your party handles mana, and test it in the [Mana Calculator](../tools/mana-calculator.md) first.
- **Attack Speed Omens:** these get dangerous when they stack. <span class="pdx-status pdx-status--observed" title="Seen in play or testing. No official source.">Observed</span> Stack too many in the Heist at Smuggler's Wharf, and an enemy can become impossible to kill.

## Planning a run

**Save up VITA as a group.** Hold off on running decks until someone in the group hits the [80 VITA cap](../getting-started/core-concepts.md#vita), then wait one more day. Steps past the cap still get added for up to two days, so that extra day costs nothing. Then run your decks back to back. You'll get through more decks in one sitting and spend less time in waystones overall. That leaves the rest of your time free for Trading Posts and putting quest experience into other classes.

Shrine buffs last 24 hours, so run your decks in order:

1. **Shrines first.** Build your first deck to stack as many Shrines as you can.
2. **Treasures next.** While those buffs are still active, build your next decks around Treasures.

You get the full value of the buffs and more out of every Treasure deck, all while getting the most attempts at Uniques for the least VITA.

## Field notes

- <span class="pdx-status pdx-status--observed" title="Seen in play or testing. No official source.">Observed</span> A rune's first word usually names one of its Fortunes, which makes Treasure and Shrine runes quick to spot. Look for **Fortune** and **Hallowed** (Wild cards become Treasures or Shrines), **Abundant**, **Gleaming** and **Opulent** (Treasure boosts), and **Sacred** (Shrine boost). It isn't always reliable, and Omen names don't follow any pattern, so check the actual modifiers before you keep or sell a rune.
- <span class="pdx-status pdx-status--observed" title="Seen in play or testing. No official source.">Observed</span> Shrine buffs that change a deck, like turning Wild cards into Treasures or bringing more monsters of a certain rarity, seem to apply only for the player who starts the waystone or clicks **Venture Deeper**. It hasn't been properly tested, but many experienced players believe it. It costs nothing to decide as a group who starts each deck and level, so the player with the right buffs is the one clicking.
