## Preferred gear drops

Gloomhaven leans toward **Dexterity-based** gear. It's not a guarantee, and some Strength and Intellect gear still drops here, but most of what you find will be Dexterity-based.

- **Wands** only drop in Gloomhaven.

## Waystone decks

**Unique Treasures** give a random item from their reward pool. Every player opens their own, so there's no competition for these.

**Unique Monsters** drop 1 to 3 pieces of gear, depending on [party size](../../parties/party-size.md).
