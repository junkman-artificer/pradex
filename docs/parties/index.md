# Parties :lucide-triangle-alert:{ .lone-wolf-warn title="Ends Lone Wolf status" }

Grouping up makes some content easier and some rewards better.

!!! warning "Lone Wolf players"
    Joining a party ends your [Lone Wolf](../character-creation/index.md#lone-wolf-optional) status.

## Forming a party

- **Be in the same area.** You can only join a party with players in the same area as you.
- **Turn on Looking for Party** to get invites from anyone. Open the side menu, go to **Friends**, and switch it on. With it off, only your friends can invite you.

## The party leader

Whoever sends the invites becomes the **party leader** and gets a crown icon. As far as anyone can tell, being leader doesn't change anything.

There's no way to hand leadership to someone else. If the leader leaves, it seems to pass to the member who's been in the party the longest.

## Party size and rewards

Bigger parties get more gear from Unique Monsters, but 3 and 4 player parties cost more Vita per fight. See [Party size and gear drops](../waystones/maximizing.md#party-size-and-gear-drops) for the full breakdown.

Prado doesn't have traditional roles like tank, healer, and damage. Instead, certain class traits and abilities excel at different jobs, so it's worth thinking about how your party's classes cover each other.

## Ability synergies

Some abilities work especially well together across classes.

### The Mana Loop

**Priest + Magician:** Prayer of Healing + Arcane Infusion

Prayer of Healing instantly restores health and mana to all allies, and Arcane Infusion gives all allies mana regen over 3 seconds. Each one refills the other caster's mana, so the pair can keep each other casting much longer than either could alone.

|---|---|---|---|
| I | Level 11 | 9 health, 5 mana | 12 |

| IV | Level 25, Tier 4 | 64 health, 40 mana | 52 |
