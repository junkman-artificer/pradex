# Building a Team

Prado doesn't really have traditional roles like tank, healer, and damage. Instead, certain class traits and abilities are great at different jobs, so it's worth thinking about how your party's classes cover each other.

A good team covers three things, in whatever mix its classes bring:

1. **Stay standing.** Everything that keeps your party alive: healing, regen, Barrier, damage reduction, and control like stuns, freezes, slows and sleep, since a stunned enemy isn't hitting anyone. Think of the Priest's heals, the Bard's Meadowland Pastoral, the Warrior's Intimidating Shout, or the Magician's Blizzard.
2. **End it fast.** Everything that shortens the fight before fatigue or enemy healing can catch up: raw damage, damage buffs like the Warrior's War Cry or the Bard's Soldier's Fanfare, haste, and debuffs that make enemies take more, like the Thief's Marked for Death.
3. **Stay fueled.** Mana powers both of the above, and it usually runs out long before health does. The Magician's Arcane Infusion, the Priest's Prayer of Healing, the Bard's Harmony and Mana Regen on gear keep everyone casting through a whole deck. The [Mana Calculator](../tools/mana-calculator.md) shows how long your party can keep it up.

Most classes can cover a bit of each on their own. What sets them apart is how strong they are at it, and whether it helps the whole party or only themselves. A Thief's Leech and Extraction only refill the Thief, while a Priest's Prayer of Healing refills everyone. When you're building a team, the shared stuff goes further.

## Combos worth knowing

Most abilities just add up, but a few pairs from different classes do far more together than apart.

### The Mana Loop

**Priest + Magician:** Prayer of Healing + Arcane Infusion

Prayer of Healing instantly restores health and mana to all allies, and Arcane Infusion gives all allies mana regen over 3 seconds. Each one refills the other caster's mana, so the pair can keep each other casting much longer than either could alone.

### Deep Freeze

**Magician + Thief or Warrior:** Icebrand Weapon + Dual Wield or Whirlwind

Icebrand Weapon makes every successful front line weapon hit freeze its target, and there are two ways to make the most of it. A dual-wielding Thief lands twice the hits, so its target spends most of the fight frozen. A Warrior's Whirlwind strikes every enemy with its weapon at once, freezing the whole enemy side. Freeze has diminishing returns when it's reapplied over and over, so it won't hold anyone forever, but it still takes a big chunk of the fight away from them.

### Dodge Tank

**Warrior + Bard:** Provoke, Counter and Rage + Dancer's Bagatelle

Provoke forces enemies to attack the Warrior, and Dancer's Bagatelle raises everyone's evasion, so a lot of those attacks miss. Every dodge sets off Counter, a free attack with the main-hand weapon, and every hit that lands builds Rage, adding weapon damage each time. Add Mighty Strikes, and every attack also splashes damage onto adjacent targets, turning each counter into a small AoE.

### Spell Storm

**Bard + Magician:** Sorcerer's Lament, Wizard's Minuet and Moonlight Rhapsody + any spells

Sorcerer's Lament lowers the enemy's magic resistance, Wizard's Minuet raises every ally's spellpower, and Moonlight Rhapsody makes their spells cheaper. The Magician hits harder and gets more spells out of the same mana. A Priest's Holy Nova and Penance get the same boost.

!!! tip "Bigger groups, bigger value"
    Enemies scale with your party, so a group of 3 or 4 faces more monsters at once. Anything that hits the whole enemy side, like Blizzard or Explosive Shot, gets stronger as your group grows. The exception is a pure damage dealer with **Focus Target**, the Thief's level 8 trait, which any class can pick up through a Thief subclass. It locks onto one enemy at a time and thins out a crowd faster than spreading damage around.

Coordinated groups can also split up crafting professions and pass each other the materials they find, so everyone levels their specialty faster.
