# Strategy

Tricks and priorities for getting the most out of your attunements. For how enchanting works, see [Enchanting](index.md).

## Replace or reroll?

**Reroll the value** when the affix is one you want but its number is sitting low. It's the safe option: you keep the affix and its tier, and since a reroll can't land on the value you already have, a tier with only two values is a guaranteed upgrade.

**Replace the affix** when it's the wrong stat for your build, or when its tier is so low that even a max roll won't be worth much. It's also the only way to reach a higher tier. You're offered three random choices, and if none of them are worth taking, you can back out and keep what you had. The catch is that the item is stuck until you pick one: no more enchanting and no imbuing.

!!! tip "Save the risky replace for last"
    On a Rare, make your final attunement the replace. If all three choices are worse than what you have, just leave them. Your current affix stays, and since the item is out of attunements anyway, being stuck costs you nothing.

    This only works when replacing the whole affix. A value reroll takes effect right away, so there's nothing to back out of.

## Before you imbue

Uncommon affixes always roll from the upper half of their tiers, but Rare affixes don't. And when you imbue an Uncommon to Rare, **both of its affixes carry over** unchanged.

That makes Uncommon the best place to set up your item. Spend every Uncommon attunement locking in two strong affixes and maxing their values, then imbue to Rare. You'll keep those high-floor affixes, gain new slots, and get fresh attunements to work on the rest.

## The 6th socket

On high-level gear, a strong Prefix can be worth more than a 6th socket. See [Is the 6th socket worth it?](../gearing-up/rarity.md#socketed) on the Rarity page.

## More tricks

- **Borrow a subscriber's attunements.** Subscribers get 2 extra attunements on every item. If you're not a subscriber but a friend is, have them enchant the item for you before you equip it. Once it's bound to you, it's too late.
- **Don't hoard spare gear.** Disenchant it for shards and essence, or sell the promising pieces that just didn't roll what you wanted on the Trading Post.
- **Keep your crafting professions leveled.** Imbuing takes crafting materials for that type of gear, like Linen Cloth for a Linen Shirt. A profession that keeps pace with your level means you can make those materials yourself instead of hunting for them.
- **Reduced Attribute Requirements lets you wear gear early.** It's an affix on most weapons and armor, and it can let you equip a strong piece a few levels before you'd normally meet its stat requirement.
