# Strategy

Tricks and priorities for getting the most out of your attunements. For how enchanting works, see [Enchanting](index.md).

## Replace or reroll?

[When to replace an affix entirely vs. reroll its value.]

!!! tip "Free upgrades near the top of a range"
    A value reroll can't land on the value you already have. If your enchantment tier only has two values and you started with the lower one, rerolling is a guaranteed upgrade.

## Before you imbue

Imbuing refills your attunements, so spend every Uncommon attunement first. Use them to land strong starting affixes or max out values, then imbue to Rare and start fresh.

## The 6th socket

On high-level gear, a strong Prefix can be worth more than a 6th socket. See [Is the 6th socket worth it?](../gearing-up/rarity.md#socketed) on the Rarity page.
