# Arena :lucide-triangle-alert:{ .lone-wolf-warn title="Ends Lone Wolf status" }

The Arena is Prado's ranked player vs. player mode. Teams of three duke it out for rating, titles, and, in the top league, Marks of Valor. It unlocks at **level 7**.

!!! warning "Lone Wolf players"
    Entering the Arena ends your [Lone Wolf](../character-creation/index.md#lone-wolf-optional) status.

## How matches work

- **Matches are instant.** There's no queue. When you enter a match, you're paired with a **ghost**: a snapshot of another team, saved right after that team's last match. Your own team is saved the same way and becomes a ghost for other players to fight.
- **Fights are automatic,** using the same rules as everywhere else. See [Combat](../combat/index.md) for formations, targeting, and fatigue.
- **Draws:** if both teams go down at once, your rating doesn't change.

Arena teams still use the 2x2 [formation](../combat/index.md#formations) grid. With three players, one slot is left empty, either in the front line or the back line.

## Arena teams

### Forming a team

1. Open the **Arena** and go to the **Halls of Valor**.
2. Pick your league from the level tabs at the top (Lv 1 to 9, Lv 10 to 19, or Lv 20 to 25).
3. Under **Your Arena Teams**, tap an empty team slot.
4. Give your team a name (up to 32 characters) and invite players into the two empty player slots. You can only invite people on your **friends list**.
5. Each player who accepts joins the roster. Once all three slots are filled, tap **Create Party** to finalize the team.

You can be on up to **two Arena teams** at a time, and one team can compete in more than one league.

### Personal and team rating

The Halls of Valor tracks two kinds of rating:

- **Your personal rating:** one per league, shown at the top with your rank and your highest rating this season.
- **Your team's rating:** each team has its own matchmaking rating, so your two teams can sit at very different ratings.

### Your PvP build

Your Arena build is **separate from your PvE build**. It can use a different class and different gear, but it doesn't update on its own. So when you pick up new gear, don't forget to update your PvP build by hand too.

!!! warning "Shrine and potion buffs stay with their class"
    Shrine blessings and potions are tied to the class that received them. If you play a different class in the Arena than in PvE, those buffs won't carry over.

## Leagues

The Arena is split into three leagues by level. Your team's league is set by its **highest-level member**.

| League | Levels |
|---|---|
| Skirmisher | 1 to 9 |
| Duelist | 10 to 19 |
| Gladiator | 20 to 25 |

For example, a team with two level 9 players and one level 15 player competes in the **Duelist** League.

Each league has its own rating and its own leaderboard.

## Ranks and titles

Your rank in each league depends on your rating there.

| Rating | Rank |
|---|---|
| 0 to 199 | Recruit |
| 200 to 399 | Squire |
| 400 to 799 | Knight |
| 800 to 1,199 | Challenger |
| 1,200+ | Skirmisher, Duelist, or Gladiator |

Reaching the top rank in a league earns that league's exclusive PvP title: **Skirmisher**, **Duelist**, or **Gladiator**.

Your **rank title never drops**. It stays at the highest rank you've reached, even if your rating falls. Hit Gladiator and then slip back to 1,100? You're still a Gladiator, and you can still buy the Gladiator weapons.

## Marks of Valor

Marks of Valor are the Arena's currency, spent on special Unique items.

!!! warning "Gladiator League only"
    Only matches in the **Gladiator League** give Marks of Valor. Skirmisher and Duelist matches give rating and ranks, but no Marks.

| Result | Marks of Valor |
|---|---|
| Win, +24 rating or less | 50 |
| Win, +25 rating | 50 or 55 |
| Win, +26 rating or more | 55 |
| Loss | 20 |
| Draw | [ ] |

Bigger rating gains come from **upset wins**, beating a team you were expected to lose to, so those are the wins that pay 55. The cutoff sits right at +25. The game probably tracks rating more precisely than the whole number it shows, so a +25 on screen can land on either side.

## Arena Uniques

<span class="rarity rarity--unique">Unique</span> items sold at **Valor's Armory** in the Halls of Valor, bought with Marks of Valor. Which ones you can buy depends on your Arena rank, which is always the highest rank you've reached. Every item is **level 25** and **Soulbound**.

Every Arena Unique gives **Health**, **Mana**, and **% All Hit Rate**. Armor pieces add **Armor**, and weapons add weapon damage. They have no other stats, so they're weaker than a well-rolled item. The armor and jewelry have **no attribute requirements**, though, which makes them a good way to bulk up a squishy character.

### Weapons

All three weapons need **Gladiator** rank, cost **800** Marks, and give **+500 Health**, **+300 Mana**, and **+6% All Hit Rate**. Unlike the armor, each one has an attribute requirement.

| Item | Type | Physical damage | Speed | Requires |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Gladiator's Shiv | Dagger | 42 to 45 | 240 | 60 Intellect |
| Gladiator's Cutlass | Sword | 55 to 60 | 320 | 60 Dexterity |
| Gladiator's War Axe | Axe | 88 to 94 | 500 | 60 Strength |

!!! tip "Stock refreshes daily"
    The Armory stocks one of each item, and the stock refreshes **every day**. Lost something, or want a second copy? Just come back tomorrow. Uniques aren't limited to one per character either, so go ahead and wear a pair of Gladiator's Bands or two of the same weapon.

## Tips

**PvP priorities aren't PvE priorities.** A general set of gear does just fine in PvE, but the Arena asks for things you never really have to worry about there:

- **Big bursts of damage.** Make sure you can outlast heavy spikes, not just steady damage.
- **Layered crowd control.** Expect stuns, freezes, and other effects stacked on top of each other. See [Status Effects](../combat/status-effects.md) for what each one does and how to protect against it.
- **Accuracy.** Check that your hit rate is high enough to reliably land attacks on enemy players.
- **Cooldown reduction.** Right now, Arena fights are usually won by whoever bursts first. Faster cooldowns get your biggest abilities out before the other team's. See the cooldown stats under [Offense](../gearing-up/stats.md#offense).
