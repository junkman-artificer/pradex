# Rarity

Gear comes in six tiers. The higher the tier, the more affixes (Prefixes and Suffixes) an item can hold, and the more chances you get to roll them.

| Tier | Prefix / Suffix slots | Attunements |
|---|---|---|
| <span class="rarity rarity--common">Common</span> | 0 / 0 | None |
| <span class="rarity rarity--socketed">Socketed</span> | 0 / 0 | None |
| <span class="rarity rarity--uncommon">Uncommon</span> | 1 / 1 | 1 to 2 |
| <span class="rarity rarity--rare">Rare</span> | 3 / 3 | 2 to 4 |
| <span class="rarity rarity--epic">Epic</span> | 3 / 3 | Unlimited |
| <span class="rarity rarity--unique">Unique</span> | Fixed | None |

Subscribers get 2 extra attunements on every item.

!!! note "Slot exceptions"
    - **Shields** can be found Socketed, but can't roll the Sockets Prefix.
    - **Orbs, Relics, Instruments, Quivers, Shoulders, Capes, Rings, and Necklaces** can't be Socketed and can't roll the Sockets Prefix.

## What are attunements?

An **attunement** is one roll when enchanting. You can spend it in one of two ways:

- **Reroll the affix:** get three new random options, each with its own affix, tier, and value, and pick one to replace the current affix.
- **Reroll the value:** keep the affix and its tier, and roll for a new value within that tier's range. For example, if your affix's tier has a 9 to 12% range, you can reroll it in hopes of hitting 12%.

There's no way to reroll just the tier. A higher tier only comes from getting lucky on a full affix reroll.

Once an item runs out of attunements, its affixes are locked in until you imbue it to the next tier. Rare is the highest tier you can imbue to, so a Rare's final rolls are permanent. For more details, see [Enchanting](../enchanting/index.md).

## The tiers

### <span class="rarity rarity--common">Common</span>

No affix slots and no attunements. What you see is what you get, unless you imbue it.

### <span class="rarity rarity--socketed">Socketed</span>

A Common-grade item with built-in sockets, up to **5**. Built-in sockets don't use any affix slots and stay with the item as it's imbued to higher tiers.

!!! tip "The endgame path"
    1. Find a Socketed item with the full **5 sockets**.
    2. Imbue it up to Uncommon, then Rare. It keeps all 5 sockets and gains the full 3 Prefix and 3 Suffix slots.
    3. Roll its affixes well. You can roll the **Sockets** Prefix to reach the cap of **6 usable sockets**, but it isn't always the best use of that Prefix slot.

    According to the developers, a Socketed item built this way can, in theory, end up stronger than any Epic.

!!! info "Is the 6th socket worth it?"
    A stat gem tops out at **+5** to a stat, Health Regen, or Mana Regen, or **15%** increased item rarity. On high-level gear, a strong Prefix roll (like a boost to weapon damage on a weapon) can be worth more than one more gem. Compare what that Prefix slot could roll against what a gem would add before committing to it.

### <span class="rarity rarity--uncommon">Uncommon</span>

Holds up to 1 Prefix and 1 Suffix. What it lacks in slots, it makes up for in quality: Uncommon affixes always roll from the **upper half** of the tiers available to that item, including when you enchant it.

Crafted gear always starts as Uncommon with both slots filled. Uncommon gear from other sources sometimes has only one, and you can fill the empty slot with enchanting.

### <span class="rarity rarity--rare">Rare</span>

Holds up to 3 Prefixes and 3 Suffixes. Rare items don't get Uncommon's higher affix floor, so there's much more variance. You have more slots to work with, but landing exactly the rolls you want is harder.

A well-rolled Rare can handle pretty much everything, which makes it the realistic goal for most players.

### <span class="rarity rarity--epic">Epic</span>

Same slots as Rare, but with **unlimited attunements**, so you can keep rolling until it's exactly what you want (as long as you have the materials).

Epics are rarer than Uniques and can't be farmed. They only show up as random monster drops, and just a handful are found each season. Enjoy one if you find it, but don't plan around it.

### <span class="rarity rarity--unique">Unique</span>

Comes as-is, with no attunements. Uniques are often extremely powerful or have special effects you won't find on other items of their type.

Despite the name, you can equip more than one copy of the same Unique, like two of the same ring or two of the same weapon.
