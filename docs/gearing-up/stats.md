# Stats

What each stat does, grouped by what it helps with. For exact tiers, roll odds, and which stats can appear on which item types, use the official enchantments page.

[:lucide-sparkles: Official enchantments list](https://www.pradotraveler.com/enchantments){ .md-button .md-button--primary target="_blank" }

Within each group, stats are split into **Prefixes** and **Suffixes**. That matters when you're enchanting, since each item has a limited number of slots of each type.

Descriptions come from the in-game character sheet where possible. Anything still marked `[ ]` hasn't been confirmed yet.

## Core attributes

Strength, Dexterity, and Intellect each boost several other stats at once, and they also decide which gear you can equip.

**Prefixes**

| Stat | What it does |
|---|---|
| Strength | +8 Max Health per point. Every 8 points adds 1 physical weapon damage. |
| Dexterity | +0.25% chance to hit and to critically strike per point. |
| Intellect | +5 Max Mana per point. Every 8 points adds 1 Spell Power and 1 magical weapon damage. |
| 1 Weapon Damage for Every 10 Strength | Adds 1 weapon damage for every 10 Strength you have. |
| 1 Weapon Damage for Every 10 Dexterity | Adds 1 weapon damage for every 10 Dexterity you have. |
| 1 Weapon Damage for Every 10 Intellect | Adds 1 weapon damage for every 10 Intellect you have. |
| % Reduced Attribute Requirements | [ ] |

## Defense

Ways to avoid, absorb, or reduce incoming damage.

**Prefixes**

| Stat | What it does |
|---|---|
| % Block Rate | Chance to block an attack. A block cuts the damage in half. |
| Block Value | Additional damage blocked on top of the 50% when you block. |
| Immune to Poison, Freeze, Burn, Bleed, or Blind | Prevents that status effect entirely. Only rolls on Shields. See [Status Effects](../combat/status-effects.md). |

**Suffixes**

| Stat | What it does |
|---|---|
| Armor | Resistance to physical damage. Your character sheet shows the % it currently blocks against a monster of your level. |
| % Evasion | Chance to evade an attack from an enemy of your level. |
| Barrier | Absorbs damage before your health is hit. Starts full each fight, and damage to it isn't reduced by armor or resistances. Most common on Intellect-based armor. See [Barrier](../combat/index.md#barrier). |
| Barrier Regen | Barrier restored every 5 seconds. |
| % of Max HP as Barrier | [ ] |
| % of Max MP as Barrier | [ ] |
| % Magic Resistance | Reduces damage taken from magical attacks. Everyone starts with 20%. |
| Physical Damage Taken Reduced by | Reduces physical damage taken by a flat amount. |
| Magical Damage Taken Reduced by | Reduces magic damage taken by a flat amount, before resistances. |
| Damage Taken Reduced | Reduces all damage taken by a flat amount. |
| Return Damage on Block | Damages the attacker when you block. |
| Return Damage on Hit | Damages the attacker when they hit you. |
| % Reduced Status Effect Debuff Duration | Shortens negative status effects on you. See [Status Effects](../combat/status-effects.md). |

## Offense

Hitting more often, harder, and faster.

**Prefixes**

| Stat | What it does |
|---|---|
| Damage | [ ] |
| Frost Spell Power | Frost spells deal that much extra damage. Every 4 points adds 0.2 seconds to Freeze. |
| Fire Spell Power | Fire spells deal that much extra damage. Every 4 points adds 1 Burn damage. |
| Arcane Spell Power | Arcane spells deal that much extra damage. Every 4 points lowers mana costs by 1. |
| Holy Spell Power | Holy spells deal that much extra damage. Every 2 points adds 1 to the health your healing spells restore. |
| Performance | Each point adds 0.2 seconds to song-based Haste and Slow effects. |

!!! info "Where Spell Power and Performance come from"
    - **Spell Power** can be rolled as a Prefix on certain gear, most often Staves and Orbs. Check the [enchanting calculator](https://www.pradotraveler.com/enchantments) to see which items can roll each element. Every 8 Intellect also adds 1 Spell Power.
    - **Performance** only comes from three places: the Bard's level 8 **Performer** trait (every 8 Dexterity and 8 Intellect gives 1 Performance), a Prefix on an **Instrument**, or a few key Unique items from waystones.

**Suffixes**

| Stat | What it does |
|---|---|
| Weapon Damage | [ ] |
| Physical Damage | Physical attacks and abilities deal that much extra damage. |
| % Physical Damage | [ ] |
| Magical Damage | Magic attacks and abilities deal that much extra damage. |
| % Magical Damage | [ ] |
| % Weapon Hit Rate | Weapon hit chance against an enemy of your level. |
| % Spell Hit Rate | Spell hit chance against an enemy of your level. |
| % All Hit Rate | Raises both weapon and spell hit chance. |
| % Weapon Crit Rate | Chance to critically strike with your weapon. |
| % Spell Crit Rate | Chance to critically strike with spells. |
| % All Crit Rate | Raises both weapon and spell crit chance. |
| % All Crit Multiplier | The multiplier applied to damage or healing when you critically strike. |
| % of Max Mana as Weapon Damage | [ ] |
| % of Max Health as Spell Damage | [ ] |
| % Weapon Damage Cleave | Also hits enemies in the same row as your target. Rolls on Clubs, Great Axes, Great Swords, Maces, and Staves. |
| % Weapon Damage Pierce | Also hits enemies in the same column as your target, punching through the front line to whoever is behind. Rolls on Bows and Spears. |
| % Weapon Damage Splash | Also hits enemies in the same row and column as your target. Only rolls on Clubs. |
| % Chance to Double Strike | Chance for a weapon attack to strike twice. Only rolls on Bows. |
| % Chance to Triple Strike | Chance for a weapon attack to strike three times. Only rolls on Bows. |
| Add % Weapon Damage Dealt to Barrier | [ ] |
| Add % Spell Damage Dealt to Barrier | [ ] |
| Add % Crit Damage to Barrier | [ ] |
| Decrease Weapon Cooldown by | Shortens the time between weapon attacks. Lower is better. |
| % Faster Weapon Cooldowns | Shortens the time between weapon attacks by a percentage. |
| % Faster Ability Cooldown | Shortens the cooldowns of your active abilities. Shown as "Ability Speed" on your character sheet. |
| All Cooldowns % Faster | Shortens both ability cooldowns and the time between weapon attacks. Shown as "All Speed" on your character sheet. |

## Status effects

Chances to apply statuses, and bonus damage against targets that have them. For what each status does, see [Status Effects](../combat/status-effects.md).

**Prefixes**

| Stat | What it does |
|---|---|
| Poison Potency | Each point adds 1 Poison stack to your poisoning abilities. For example, +2 makes a level 1 Poisoned Dagger apply 3 stacks. Only rolls on Daggers. |
| Poison Duration | Makes the Poison you apply last longer. |

**Suffixes**

| Stat | What it does |
|---|---|
| % Chance to Apply Bleed | Chance for your attacks to apply Bleed. |
| % Chance to Apply Poison | Chance for your attacks to apply Poison. |
| % Chance to Apply Burn | Chance for your attacks to apply Burn. |
| % Chance to Apply Freeze | Chance for your attacks to apply Freeze. |
| % Chance to Apply Stun | Chance for your attacks to apply Stun. |
| % Chance to Apply Blind | Chance for your attacks to apply Blind. |
| % Chance to Sunder Armor | Chance for your attacks to apply Sunder Armor. |
| % Chance to Hamstring | Chance for your attacks to apply Hamstring. |
| % Chance to Deep Wound | Chance for your attacks to apply Deep Wound. |
| % Chance to Curse | Chance for your attacks to apply Curse. |
| % Chance to Enfeeble | Chance for your attacks to apply Enfeeble. |
| % Bonus Damage to Poisoned Targets | Extra damage against Poisoned targets. |
| % Bonus Damage to Bleeding Targets | Extra damage against Bleeding targets. |
| % Bonus Damage to Frozen Targets | Extra damage against Frozen targets. |
| % Bonus Damage to Stunned Targets | Extra damage against Stunned targets. |
