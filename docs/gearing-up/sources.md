# Where to Get Gear

There are six ways to get gear. Most players use all of them at different points.

## Monster drops

[What normal monsters drop while leveling.]

Unique Monsters in waystones drop 1 to 3 pieces of gear, depending on [party size](../parties/party-size.md). Monster drops are also the only way to find an <span class="rarity rarity--epic">Epic</span>.

## Treasure

[Treasure found while leveling.]

Unique Treasures in waystones give a random item from their reward pool, and every player opens their own. See each [region's page](../waystones/regions/index.md) for what's in its pools.

## Crafting

Crafted gear always comes out <span class="rarity rarity--uncommon">Uncommon</span> with both its Prefix and Suffix filled. That's the max for Uncommon, which makes crafting the most reliable way to get a complete Uncommon item.

[:lucide-arrow-right: Full guide: Crafting](../crafting/index.md)

## Arena :lucide-triangle-alert:{ .lone-wolf-warn title="Ends Lone Wolf status" }

Special <span class="rarity rarity--unique">Unique</span> items with fixed stats, bought with **Marks of Valor**. The armor and jewelry have no attribute requirements. Marks only come from Gladiator League matches.

[:lucide-arrow-right: Full guide: Arena](../arena/index.md)
