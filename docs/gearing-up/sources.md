## Monster drops

[What normal monsters drop while leveling.]

Unique Monsters in waystones drop 1 to 3 pieces of gear, depending on [party size](../waystones/maximizing.md#party-size-and-gear-drops). Monster drops are also the only way to find an <span class="rarity rarity--epic">Epic</span>.

## Treasure

[Treasure found while leveling.]

Unique Treasures in waystones give a random item from their reward pool, and every player opens their own. See each [region's page](../waystones/regions/index.md) for what's in its pools.

## Crafting

Crafted gear always comes out <span class="rarity rarity--uncommon">Uncommon</span> with both its Prefix and Suffix filled. That's the max for Uncommon, which makes crafting the most reliable way to get a complete Uncommon item.

## Arena :lucide-triangle-alert:{ .lone-wolf-warn title="Ends Lone Wolf status" }

Special <span class="rarity rarity--unique">Unique</span> items can be bought with **Marks of Valor**, and which ones are available depends on your Arena rank.

They always come with the same four stats: Health, Mana, Armor, and % All Hit Rate. That makes them weaker than a well-rolled item, but they have **no attribute requirements**, so they can be a good way to bulk up a squishy character. The tradeoff is that they have no other stats.
