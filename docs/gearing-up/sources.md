# Where to Get Gear

There are six ways to get gear. Most players use all of them at different points.

## Town stores

Town stores sell randomly generated, enchanted gear that's level-appropriate for the region.

- **Eastern Gate:** each class has its own shop, and each shop offers 3 pieces of gear.
- **Other towns:** every class's gear is mixed into one stock, so you never know what will be for sale, or whether there will be anything for the slot you're trying to upgrade.

**When they're worth it:**

- **The right enchantment.** Early **Mana Regen** is worth its weight in gold, and **Restore Mana on Enemy Defeated** isn't far behind.
- **Two-handed weapons.** They're a lot rarer as monster drops before waystones, so a store can be the easiest place to find one.
- **Skipping a craft.** Buy an <span class="rarity rarity--uncommon">Uncommon</span> and [imbue](../enchanting/index.md#imbuing) it up to <span class="rarity rarity--rare">Rare</span> for a solid working piece, without spending materials crafting the base item.

## Monster drops

Monsters can drop almost anything: junk, gear, spellbooks, gems, and crafting materials.

Only certain monsters can drop a <span class="rarity rarity--unique">Unique</span> tied to them. When one can, it's usually listed in the **Loot** tab of that monster's information.

!!! tip "The Masked Revenant and the Ring of Despair"
    The **Masked Revenant** is an Uncommon monster in **Eastern Gate** who can show up in any adventure deck there except **Old City Forum**. He can drop the **Ring of Despair**, a Unique ring with an unbelievable **+4 Mana Regen**, and it isn't listed in his Loot tab.

    If he drops it, it goes straight to one player instead of showing up in Party Loot to roll on. It's also Soulbound, so the only way to get one is to get lucky.

Unique Monsters in waystones drop 1 to 3 pieces of gear, depending on [party size](../parties/party-size.md). Monster drops are also the only way to find an <span class="rarity rarity--epic">Epic</span>.

## Treasure

As you click through the cards in a deck, a wild card can sometimes turn up as a **treasure card**. That lets you claim a treasure box to open. See the [FAQ](../getting-started/faq.md) for a faster way to open them.

Two decks also give a treasure box when you complete them. Each box holds one of three <span class="rarity rarity--unique">Unique</span> items.

### Skeleton's Chest

From **The Bone Gallery** in [Eastern Gate](../waystones/regions/eastern-gate.md). All three items are level 9.

| Item | Type | Damage | Speed | Bonuses | Requires |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Crypt Raider's Axe | Axe | 11 to 14 physical | 400 | +3 Strength, +2 Health Regen | 10 Strength |
| Catacomb Staff | Staff (two-handed) | 10 to 11 magical | 380 | +3 Intellect, +3 Mana Regen, +2 Holy Spell Power | 10 Intellect |
| Cryptkeeper's Lantern | Relic (off-hand) | None | None | +1 Intellect, +1 Mana Regen, +2 Holy Spell Power | 10 Intellect |

### Goblin Treasure Box

From **Thronheart Chamber** in [Gloomhaven](../waystones/regions/gloomhaven.md). All three items are level 16.

| Item | Type | Damage | Speed | Bonuses | Requires |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Goblin's Bane Sword | Sword | 14 to 18 physical | 320 | +3 Strength, +3 Dexterity, +8 Health on Kill, +12 Mana on Kill | 24 Strength, 24 Dexterity |
| Gloomwood Longbow | Bow (two-handed) | 30 to 36 physical | 430 | +5 Dexterity, +5% Weapon Hit, +5% Weapon Crit, 7% Double Strike | 27 Dexterity |
| Fungal Mystic's Staff | Staff (two-handed) | 20 to 24 magical | 370 | +2 Strength, +4 Intellect, +3 Mana Regen, +3 Health on Spell Hit, +3 Mana on Spell Hit | 27 Intellect |

Unique Treasures in waystones give a random item from their reward pool, and every player opens their own. See each [region's page](../waystones/regions/index.md) for what's in its pools.

## Crafting

Crafted gear always comes out <span class="rarity rarity--uncommon">Uncommon</span> with both its Prefix and Suffix filled. That's the max for Uncommon, which makes crafting the most reliable way to get a complete Uncommon item.

[:lucide-arrow-right: Full guide: Crafting](../crafting/index.md)

## Arena :lucide-triangle-alert:{ .lone-wolf-warn title="Ends Lone Wolf status" }

Special <span class="rarity rarity--unique">Unique</span> items with fixed stats, bought with **Marks of Valor**. The armor and jewelry have no attribute requirements. Marks only come from Gladiator League matches.

[:lucide-arrow-right: Full guide: Arena](../arena/index.md)

## Trading Post :lucide-triangle-alert:{ .lone-wolf-warn title="Ends Lone Wolf status" }

Buy gear from other players. It unlocks once you reach **Eastern Gate**. Weapons and armor each get their own listing at a set price, and you can filter by item level, stat requirements, enchantments, sockets, and more.

[:lucide-arrow-right: Full guide: Trading Post](../economy/index.md#trading-post)

!!! warning "Lone Wolf players"
    Using the Arena or the Trading Post ends your [Lone Wolf](../character-creation/index.md#lone-wolf-optional) status.
