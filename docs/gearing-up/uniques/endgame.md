# Endgame Uniques

Every Unique from the waystones, plus three late leveling bosses whose gear stays strong all the way up. For how to farm waystones, see [Maximizing Waystones](../../waystones/maximizing.md).

**Unique Treasures** give a random item from their reward pool, and every player opens their own. **Unique Monsters** drop 1 to 3 pieces of gear, depending on [party size](../../parties/party-size.md).
