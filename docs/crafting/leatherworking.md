# Leatherworking

[:lucide-slice: Official Leatherworking page](https://www.pradotraveler.com/leatherworking){ .md-button .md-button--primary target="_blank" }

Leatherworking turns leather and thread into leather armor, capes, and quivers. Recipes are grouped by the leather they use, from the earliest tier to the latest.

Every Leatherworking recipe also uses **1 Earth Crystal**, so it's left out of the tables below.

**Required level** is approximate (marked with ~). In testing, it's usually the item level or one higher.

**Attribute required** is shown as a range, because the exact requirement varies from one crafted item to the next.

Recipes marked :lucide-scroll: aren't learned automatically. You have to find their design first. Hover over or tap the icon to see where it drops.

!!! warning "You'll need Tailoring too"
    Each leather tier needs the same-tier thread, and threads are made through [Tailoring](tailoring.md). Plan to level Tailoring alongside Leatherworking, or buy threads from other players.
