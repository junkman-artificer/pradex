# Smithing

[:lucide-anvil: Official Smithing page](https://www.pradotraveler.com/smithing){ .md-button .md-button--primary target="_blank" }

Smithing turns ingots into plate armor, weapons, rings, and necklaces. Recipes are grouped by the ingot they use, from the earliest tier to the latest.

Every Smithing recipe also uses **1 Fire Crystal**, so it's left out of the tables below.

**Required level** is approximate (marked with ~). In testing, it's usually the item level or one higher.

Silver, Gold, and Mithril Ingots are used for rings and necklaces.
