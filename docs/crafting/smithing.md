# Smithing

[:lucide-anvil: Official Smithing page](https://www.pradotraveler.com/smithing){ .md-button .md-button--primary target="_blank" }

Smithing turns ingots into plate armor, weapons, rings, and necklaces. Recipes are grouped by the ingot they use, from the earliest tier to the latest.

Every Smithing recipe also uses **1 Fire Crystal**, so it's left out of the tables below.

<span class="pdx-status pdx-status--observed" title="Seen in play or testing. No official source.">Observed</span> **Required level** is marked with ~. It's usually the item level or one higher.

**Attribute required** is shown as a range, because the exact requirement varies from one crafted item to the next. Armor uses STR, while weapons follow their type: DEX for swords, daggers and bows, INT for staffs, and STR for the rest. Jewelry has no requirement at all.

Recipes marked :lucide-scroll: aren't learned automatically. You have to find their plans first. Hover over or tap the icon to see where they might drop. <span class="pdx-status pdx-status--unconfirmed" title="A best guess that hasn't been tested yet.">Unconfirmed</span> These locations are based on where each metal is first found.

Silver, Gold, and Mithril Ingots are used for rings and necklaces.

## Gathering materials

<span class="pdx-status pdx-status--observed" title="Seen in play or testing. No official source.">Observed</span> There are no public loot tables for Prado Traveler, so these picks come from player experience. See [Crafting](index.md#gathering-materials) for what the :lucide-store: and :lucide-orbit: icons mean.

Ore comes from vein, deposit and cluster cards. Many adventure decks also have a vein or two.

<div class="pdx-compact" markdown>

| Ore | Best place | Region |
|---|---|---|
| Copper | Collapsed Mine Shaft | Eastern Gate |
| Tin, Silver | Flooded Ruins :lucide-move-right: Sunken Quarry | The Burrows |
| Iron, Coal, Gold | The Iron Pits :lucide-move-right: Miner's Folly :lucide-move-right: Vein of Kings | Briarstone Quarry |
| Arcanite :lucide-store:{ title="Up to 2 a day at Gale's Training Yard (Stormwatch)" } | Highwind Mines | Stormwatch |
| Mithril | Excavation Site No. 7 | Icetooth Base Camp |

</div>

- **Just need Coal?** Leave the Iron Pits triple-decker after you finish Miner's Folly.
- **Mithril:** Highwind Mines drops a few, but nothing beats Excavation Site No. 7. Getting up to Icetooth Base Camp costs 40 VITA, so it's only worth it if you plan to stay a while. The Excavation Site also drops Arcanite, but Arcanite and Mithril share drop chances there, so it's not the best pick if you only want Arcanite.
