# Crafting

There are five crafting professions. Three make gear, and two make consumables.

## Crystals

Every recipe uses at least one crystal. Gear professions always use the same one:

| Profession | Crystal |
|---|---|
| Smithing | Fire Crystal |
| Tailoring | Wind Crystal |
| Leatherworking | Earth Crystal |
| Alchemy | Varies by recipe |
| Cooking | Varies by recipe |

Every town sells crystals except Briarstone Quarry. Shops restock daily with a random amount, so these are the most each town can have:

## Gathering materials

Each profession page has its own **Gathering materials** section with the best places to farm what it needs. There are no public loot tables for Prado Traveler, so these picks come from player experience.

- :lucide-store: means you can also buy it in town. Hover over or tap the icon to see where and how many. Shops restock daily with a random amount up to a cap, so raw materials won't always be there.
- :lucide-orbit: means it only comes from waystone decks.

## Imbuing

Imbuing upgrades an item to the next rarity tier, from Socketed or Common to Uncommon, then to Rare. It uses crafting materials, which is why Crafting comes before Enchanting in this guide.

For what imbuing costs and what it adds at each step, see [Imbuing](../enchanting/index.md#imbuing).

For why this matters, see [the endgame path](../gearing-up/rarity.md#socketed) on the Rarity page.

## Leveling a profession

Crafting earns profession experience, and leveling up unlocks more advanced recipes. As you level, older recipes give less experience, so it pays to keep moving up to newer ones.

!!! tip "First craft bonus"
    The first time you craft any recipe, you get **double** its full experience, even if you've outleveled it. So try every recipe at least once, and keep an eye out for rare recipe drops!

!!! info "Pick a specialty"
    Maxing a profession takes a lot of crafting, and maxing all of them at once is a real grind. Most players do better picking one or two early, then either passing the other materials to party members or selling the extras on the [Trading Post](../economy/index.md#trading-post).
