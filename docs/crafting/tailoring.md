# Tailoring

[:lucide-scissors: Official Tailoring page](https://www.pradotraveler.com/tailoring){ .md-button .md-button--primary target="_blank" }

Tailoring turns cloth and thread into cloth armor and capes. Recipes are grouped by the cloth they use, from the earliest tier to the latest.

Every Tailoring recipe also uses **1 Wind Crystal**, so it's left out of the tables below.

Each cloth tier is paired with a thread. Leatherworkers need these same threads, so they're worth making in extra or selling.
